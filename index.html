A Discord poll can't be made by pasting a block of text. Click the + next to the message box, choose Create Poll, and paste the fields below in one at a time. Set the duration yourself.

Step 1 — poll the day

Tick the days you're willing to offer in the Days dropdown at the top, and they become the answers here. The counts are how many places are open on each — don't offer a day nobody can eat on.

Step 2 — poll the place

Once the day is settled, come back and tick just that day above. Every candidate is then somewhere that's genuinely open when you'll be there, and these three answers are the poll.

Discord caps answers at 55 characters and won't take links, which is why these are just the name and area.

Make a new Scheduled Event in Discord, set it to somewhere else / external, and paste these three into the matching boxes. You still have to set the date and time yourself — and check the winner is actually open that day.

Record it

After you've been — not when the vote closes. Record where you actually ate, even if that wasn't one of the candidates. This is the only thing keeping the cooldowns honest: an unrecorded month means the picker will happily send you back.
